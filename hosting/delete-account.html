TSL Facility Manager

Delete your account

TSL Facility Manager is the app Thick Services Ltd uses to handle maintenance requests at the buildings it manages. If you signed up as a client, you can delete your account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open TSL Facility Manager and sign in.
  2. Tap your initials at the top right to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email info@thickservices.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my TSL Facility Manager account”. We'll delete it and reply to confirm, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in and your profile: name, email address and phone number
  • Your notifications and the devices registered for them
  • Your conversations with our support team

What we keep

Maintenance requests you reported, with their photos and messages, stay with the facility team as part of the building's work records. They're no longer linked to an account you can sign in to. We may also keep information where the law requires it.

Staff and supervisors

Accounts for Thick Services staff and supervisors are set up by the company. To close one, contact your supervisor or the Thick Services office.
