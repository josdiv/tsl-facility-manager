Thanks for taking a look at TSL Facility Manager. It's used by Thick Services, a facility management company in Nigeria, together with its clients and field staff.

Reporting an issue and clocking in only work when the phone is at the facility. So you can try everything from where you are, we've set up a test facility called "App Review Demo – do not use" that covers your location. Please allow location access when asked.

1. Client (report an issue)
On the first screen, enter {CLIENT_EMAIL} and tap "Send verification code", then enter the code {CLIENT_CODE}. No email is sent for this address; the code always works. Open Report, choose "App Review Demo – do not use", add a title and description (photos are optional) and tap Submit issue. You can follow it under Requests and message the team from there.

2. Supervisor (assign and review)
Sign out from Profile, tap "Staff or supervisor? Sign in" and use {SUPERVISOR_EMAIL} / {SUPERVISOR_PASSWORD}. Clock in from the Clock tab first (every task action needs a started shift). Under Review > Team work, open the request you just made and assign it to App Review Technician.

3. Technician (do the work)
Sign in with the demo account above (App Review Technician). Clock in from the Clock tab, open the task, tap Start work, then add a note and a photo and send it for review. The supervisor can then confirm it or send it back.

Account deletion: clients can delete their account under Profile > Delete account. Staff and supervisor accounts are managed by the company in its office dashboard.
